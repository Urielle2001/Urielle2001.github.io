Development,
health, & gender

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Pittsburgh. My research focuses on development economics, with an emphasis on health and gender inequalities in Sub-Saharan Africa.

I use quasi-experimental methods — instrumental variables, difference-in-differences, and regression discontinuity designs — to identify causal effects of policies and social norms on health outcomes and economic welfare.
